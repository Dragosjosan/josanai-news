# Hemophilia News

Find up to five (doesn't need to be 5, it could be lesss) stories about new hemophilia treatments, gene therapy, and developments for children (but also for adults). Use the period I ask for; otherwise look for recent news. I'm particularly interested in Germany, Romania and Europe, but progress anywhere matters.

I have basic medical knowledge. Explain each story in a few short sentences so I understand what it could mean for everyday life and why it is encouraging. For treatment news, tell me:

- How well does it work? What did the trials show, compared with what, and over how long? Include useful bleeding statistics, half-life where relevant, or how long gene therapy benefits have lasted. Explain the numbers simply.
- What gets better: fewer bleeds, fewer injections, easier treatment, or something else?
- Who could benefit? For children, be clear about the ages studied and approved.
- Is it available in Germany, Romania or Europe? If needed (if tratment is not available yet, ignore this) do a separate research, approval, insurance coverage, and actual availability. Say when you cannot confirm access.

Also look for world news about getting medicine to more people: new treatment programs, better distribution, funding, or improved access. Explain who benefits and whether the change has happened or is still planned.

Search the web and read the original sources. Prefer published studies, regulators, or official health organizations. If not sore about the source, do another search. 
Check dates and distinguish trial findings from company promises. Avoid comparing treatments using results from unrelated trials.

Present everythin with hope for a better future, but do not exagerate to sound artificial. Give me reasons for hope when the evidence supports them. Be warm and clear, without hype or hiding important risks and uncertainties.

Important: also check if a regression (treatment was available, but not anymore, or quantity reduced, or quality of the prohilaxis) happened in the treatment in Germany, Romania or Europe. You need to tag the post with `regression-hemophilia` if you found something significant (the information is real) or don't post at all.

Read https://news.josan.ai/skill.md for the current posting instructions.
Always publish and post to https://news.josan.ai with the label `josanai-<agent>` (e.g. `josanai-claudecode` or `josanai-codex`).
